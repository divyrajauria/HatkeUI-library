Hatke UI

Ten everyday controls, each rebuilt around a physical idea: jelly, ink, string, gravity. Open one, tap it normally, then play with it.

Tap always works.

Drag, fling, hold and pluck are extras. A plain tap or keypress still does exactly what you expect.

Never wait on the fun.

The state changes the moment you act. The motion plays on top and can be interrupted at any point.

Calm when asked.

With reduced motion on, every component still works and still changes state, with the physics switched off.

  1. 01Jelly toggleThe knob is jelly on a spring. It squashes when pressed, stretches with its speed, overshoots and wobbles into place. You can grab it and throw it.
  2. 02Rubber-stamp checkboxEvery check is stamped by hand. The box sinks and pools ink while you press. On release the check slams in at a slightly different angle each time, ink spatters, and the label does a little cheer. Uncheck it and the check falls off.
  3. 03Scatter buttonThe words do the waiting. While it saves, the label breaks into dots that orbit. Success flips it to “Saved”. A failure brings the letters back scrambled, then they shake into the right order.
  4. 04Lens tabsA liquid lens slides under the words. Its front edge leaps and its back edge catches up on a spring. Letters change colour exactly where the lens passes, even halfway through a word. Hover a tab and the lens leans toward it.
  5. 05Balloon tooltipThe tooltip is a balloon on a string. It pops up from the button, bobs gently, swings when it travels to the next button, and leans toward your pointer.
  6. 06Toast pileNotes tossed onto a pile, each on a lit fuse. Toasts land crooked and bounce. A spark burns along each fuse, and the note flies off when it reaches the end. Hover or focus to fan the pile out and pause every fuse. Throw a note away.
  7. 07Pluck sliderThe track is a guitar string. Drag the thumb and the string bends under it. Pull up or down while you drag, let go, and it twangs and settles. The number shakes with it.
  8. 08Gravity fieldLetters have weight. Each character drops into the field and lands with a bounce. Delete one and it falls out of the bottom. A character the field can't take bounces off and flies away.
  9. 09Spring drawersDrawers on springs. Each one opens with a little overshoot, so the drawers below bump and settle. With a mouse you can grab a header and pull the drawer open, and it snaps the rest of the way.
  10. 10Squish stepperThe buttons push the number. Each press squeezes the number from that side, and the digits roll. Hold a button and it speeds up until the digits blur. At the limit the number squashes flat against the wall.
